### Game Details & Guide
Fruitninja: A Deliciously Challenging Browser Game



A fruit ninja game where players must slice fruits into precise pieces to score points, while avoiding obstacles.



Game Overview


Playing Fruitninja involves using the mouse to navigate through a maze of fruits and slices. The objective is to clear the screen by slicing as many fruits into individual pieces as possible.



How to Play (Controls and Objective)



Mouse Controls:
 Move the mouse left or right to navigate between fruit rows, then click on a fruit row to start slicing. Use your mouse wheel to zoom in and out of the screen.




Objective:
 Clear as many fruits into individual pieces as possible before time runs out. The game gets progressively harder as you progress.



Tips and Tricks for Success



Mouse Technique:
 Learn to accurately position your mouse cursor over the fruit rows without clicking on a slice, allowing you to slice multiple fruits at once.




Strategy and Patience:
 Plan ahead and think strategically about which fruits to prioritize. Focusing on clearing larger sections of fruit can help you conserve time in the long run.




Obstacle Avoidance:
 Watch out for obstacles like moving enemies that try to block your path or falling slices that will harm your progress.
